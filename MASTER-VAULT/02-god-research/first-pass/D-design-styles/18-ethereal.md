# 18 — Ethereal

> Research date: 2026-10-09 | Researcher: Styles 11-20 | Methods: WebSearch + WebFetch

## Meaning
Dreamy, airy, otherworldly aesthetic built from light diffusion, not objects. Feels weightless, calm bordering on sacred — mist / crystal light. 2026 trend reports list it under soft hues + delicate layering + blurry imagery; UXPlanet 50-styles describes it as delicacy/transcendence (design made of mist).

## Key Elements
- Diffuse pastel light: overlapping radial gradients, pastel hazes, iridescent shimmer animated across spectrum
- Feathered edges, no hard borders; frosted glass `backdrop-filter: blur` + translucent white; ambient glow orbs
- Thin airy luminous type, generous letterspacing, floating placement; soft shadows only
- Slow breathing motion; no sharp snaps; stillness as luxury
- Palette: milky whites, blush, lavender, ice blue, pearl; low contrast by intent — must still test body contrast
- Imagery: blurred / distorted photos, crystal / water metaphors, glow over grain
- Space: abundant whitespace; every surface breathes; edges dissolve into atmosphere

## Applications
- Wellness, crystal shops, boutique jewelry, tea houses, high-end craft, mindful lifestyle, psychics/healers/coaches (Ethereal Divi theme), spa retreats
- Startup hero sections wanting calm/softness vs corporate sharpness; single-page brand templates (ethereal-template)

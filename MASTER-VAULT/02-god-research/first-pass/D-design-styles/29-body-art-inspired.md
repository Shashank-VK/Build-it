# 29 — Body-art-inspired

## Meaning
No single established graphic-design style named "Body-art-inspired" found in sources — GAP stated. Term here means design drawing motifs / techniques / placement logic from body arts: tattoo (permanent ink), henna / mehndi (temporary Lawsonia inermis stain, 7–14 days, orange → reddish-brown), body paint / white henna / glitter / metallic temporaries, piercing / scarification contexts. Traditions span 5000 years: Mediterranean henna → al-Andalus dipped fingers → Persian hand/arm designs (trees/birds/stars/flowers) → Mughal/Indian bridal mehndi (1600s onward); Arabic (abstract floral/vine, less dense), African (bold geometric), Indian (lacy floral/paisley, full-hand/arm/foot, palm sun).

## Elements (as translated to design)
- Motifs: mandalas, flowers (joy/happiness), camellia, vines/leaves, paisley, dots/lines (protection), sun/moon/stars, birds/butterflies, finger-ring/chain/bangle/anklet illusions, lace-like back-of-hand work
- Line / fill: cone-drawn fine lines, dotwork, dense vs airy regional densities; brown/orange stain palette + black/white/metallic temporaries; glitter/gilding variants
- Placement logic: hands/feet/forearm/shoulder/collarbone/neck/wrist/belly emphasis; symmetry, flow along limb, jewellery-mimicry
- Application methods: freehand cone/brush, stencil cutouts, sticker transfers, carbon outlines; aftercare (avoid water 24h, oils) affects longevity — relevant when documenting process

## Uses
- Weddings/festivals (bridal hands/feet, Night of Henna), fashion/beauty editorial, festival/boho looks, logos/patterns borrowing henna linework, packaging/invitations with mehndi lace, temporary retail (kits/stencils), belly/celebration art, modern fusions combining regional styles

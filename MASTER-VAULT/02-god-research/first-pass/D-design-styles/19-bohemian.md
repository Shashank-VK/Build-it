# 19 — Bohemian (Boho / Boho-Chic)

> Research date: 2026-10-09 | Researcher: Styles 11-20 | Methods: WebSearch + WebFetch

## Meaning
Free-spirited, collected-not-curated aesthetic from 19th-c. French bohème / Roma nomad roots, popularized 1960s-70s hippie / world-travel mix. More-is-more layering, self-expression over rules. Classic boho = vibrant + eclectic; modern boho = neutral earth + edited layering. Web translation: warm, homey, storied — site feels like a home full of stories.

## Key Elements
- Earth base: terracotta, ochre, warm beige (Barely Beige, Wind's Breath), raw/medium woods (walnut, teak, rattan) + accents turquoise / chartreuse / coral / mustard / magenta
- Layered textures: linen, macramé, woven, rough paper, watercolor smudges, brush strokes, stamped ink; fringe, paisley + geometric + floral mix
- Organic soft-edged shapes, low couches metaphor (relaxed rhythm), mismatched but intentional collection; imperfection welcomed
- Typography: handwritten/script (Amelliz, Amy Riyas), decorative display (Brilon, Blackway Brush), modern boho sans for body; vintage ornamental headers
- Lighting metaphor: soft string-light / candle glow, not harsh studio; warm photography, plants, travel artifacts
- Web: rust/earth palettes, dainty custom illustrations, watercolor washes, handwritten accents, feminine/wanderlust imagery (Bold x Boho, Everleigh theme examples)

## Applications
- Boutiques, lifestyle brands, wedding/event planners, bloggers/influencers, wellness, interior brands, cafés — anywhere personality > precision
- Graphic: posters, packaging (Moroccan spice / Montmartre sunset / desert palettes), Instagram templates, music posters, editorial with vintage feel

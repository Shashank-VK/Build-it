# 07 — Neoclassical (Digital Neoclassicism / Luxury Classical)

> Style researcher 7/10. Read-only, no installs. Search-verified 2026-10-09.

## 1. Documented meaning
- Classical antiquity revival (order, symmetry, proportion, serif authority) applied to screens: museums, law, heritage luxury, haute real-estate/fashion.
- Documented in: Quantum Accord "Neo-classical" style demo (column/symmetry/serif; ivory-gold-ink; ornamental rules) — https://quantumaccordbg.com/designs/neo-classical.html — 🔍 Search-verified; Detroit UX encyclopedia art-movement lineage pattern; 1001Fonts "Neoclassical serif" tag as font-side evidence.
- Digital meaning: trust/heritage/premium pricing power; slow, deliberate pacing; restraint opposite to brutalism.

## 2. Characteristic visual elements
- Strict symmetry, centered axes, columnar grids, generous margins; golden-ratio proportions.
- High-contrast Didone/transitional serifs (Didot, Bodoni, Cormorant, Playfair), small-caps labels, letterspaced eyebrows.
- Ivory/parchment grounds, ink text, gold-foil accent, hairline rules/borders, cartouches, laurel/column motifs (SVG, restrained).
- Large editorial whitespace; numbered sections in roman numerals; restrained single-column reading measure.
- Subtle motion only: fade/rise reveals, no bounce.

## 3. Digital applications
- Law firms, private banks, auction houses/museums, luxury hotels/real-estate, heritage fashion/jewelry, wedding/editorial.

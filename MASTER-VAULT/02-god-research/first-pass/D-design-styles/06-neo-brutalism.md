# 06 — Neo-brutalism (Neubrutalism)

> Style researcher 6/10. Read-only, no installs. Search + fetch verified 2026-10-09.

## 1. Documented meaning
- Post-2020 synthesis: brutalism's rawness + friendly product design — thick black borders, hard offset shadows, flat candy colors, bold grotesque type, visible grid, playful illustration.
- Documented in: neobrutalism.dev intro ("mix of regular brutalism + modern typography/illustration/animation; refuses usual UX-UI components; fearless palettes"); Figma/community trend coverage 2022–2026; Gumroad/Figma-viral origin story.
- Digital meaning: startup-friendly memorability without brutalism's hostility; high conversion on landing/dev-tool pages when used sparingly.

## 2. Characteristic visual elements
- 2–3px black borders everywhere; hard shadows (4–8px offset, no blur); radius 0 or small pill on badges only.
- Saturated flat fills (lime, peach, lavender, sky) on off-white/paper ground; black text; one accent per block.
- Bold grotesques (Archivo, DM Sans, Space Grotesk, Lexend Mega), uppercase labels, chunky buttons.
- Sticker cards, marquee strips, starburst badges, raw tables, visible borders between sections.
- Tactile hover: translate(-2px) + shadow-grow; press: translate(+shadow) collapse.

## 3. Digital applications
- Dev-tool/SaaS landing pages, waitlists, portfolios, hackathon winners, memecoin-adjacent playfulness, docs with attitude.

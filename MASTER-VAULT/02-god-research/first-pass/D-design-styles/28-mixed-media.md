# 28 — Mixed-media

## Meaning
Mixed-media = artwork in which more than one medium / material is employed in a single piece. Not a genre but an idea / method. Began ~1912 with Cubist collages/constructions (Picasso, Braque: `Bottle of Vieux Marc, Glass, Guitar and Newspaper` 1913); widespread as attitudes opened — "essentially art can be made of anything or any combination." Distinguish from multimedia (includes time-based electronic elements: video/film/audio/computers/interactivity).

## Elements
- Combinations: paint + cloth + paper + wood + found objects; charcoal/graphite under acrylic; collage under paint; ink beside pastel; wet (paints/inks) + dry (pencil/charcoal/crayon) + digital
- Types: collage (glued papers/photos/fabric; from French `coller`), assemblage (3D collage jutting in/out or fully 3D), found-object art (Duchamp lineage), altered books, sculpture / installation (large-scale site-specific mixed-media constructions)
- Material logic: dry-unstable (charcoal, graphite, pastel — seal before wet over), stable (inks, markers, acrylic/oil/spray, resin — lock when dry), applied textures/mediums (gels, modeling/crackle paste, fabric)
- Composition principles cited for mixed-media: proportion/scale, unity/variety → harmony, contrast (light/dark, intense/neutral), balance, emphasis via theme/story/symbol

## Uses
- Fine-art painting/collage/assemblage/sculpture, editorial illustration, classroom arts-integration (collage → assemblage → sculpture progression), design textures/overlays, album / poster / packaging incorporating photo + paint + type + objects, installation art

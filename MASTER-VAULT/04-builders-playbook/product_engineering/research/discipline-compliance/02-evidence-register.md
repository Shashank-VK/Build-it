## Method note

- Official URLs used verbatim from tool results; none guessed or constructed. AICPA's criteria IDs are public; only the audit guidance is paywalled (per CSA secid commit note).
- Vendor-published figures (close rates, hours saved, costs) are direction-trusted and magnitude-discounted throughout; none is presented as an audited measurement.
- Copyrighted material: sources summarized and linked; no substantial quotation.
- Deliberately not chased: per-requirement PCI DSS QSA testing procedures, FedRAMP 20x technical drafts, per-state breach-notification interplay (out of scope for the engineering-practice brief).

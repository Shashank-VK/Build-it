## Contradictions & debates recorded

1. **Skeleton orthodoxy vs contextual loading (E2.1–E2.6):** the mainstream position (skeletons beat spinners for content loads) is challenged for unpredictable AI waits, where contextual copy beats shimmer. Resolution: match the indicator to wait *predictability*, not just duration.
2. **Toast on copy (E4.6 vs E4.7):** one school says the label change IS the feedback (no toast); another ships a 2s toast. Both agree on the ~2s revert. Resolution: pick one; never both.
3. **Undo-window seconds (E4.1–E4.3):** 5–10s undo, 3–6s info/success toasts, ≥5–6s floors — no primary study found for exact numbers. All durations treated as Proposed.
4. **Incident-update cadence (E10.13 vs E10.14):** 15 vs 30 minutes. Resolution: commit to an explicit cadence and keep it.
5. **FTC penalty figures (E10.5 vs E10.6):** $51,744 (2024) vs ~$53,088 (Dec 2025 letters) — inflation adjustment over time, not a contradiction. Always date the figure.
6. **Click-to-cancel status (E10.7 vs E10.8):** no contradiction — vacated federally, but ROSCA/§5/state laws sustain equivalent duties. The practical posture is unchanged.

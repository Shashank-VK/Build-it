## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] ≥3 independent evidence sources logged, at least one from direct observation or data (not opinion)
- [ ] Each of the four risks has an explicit verdict — no silent assumptions
- [ ] At least one non-customer / disconfirming voice recorded
- [ ] A named owner and a date for the proceed/park/kill decision

## Anti-patterns this blocks

AP-01 (weak product validation) · AP-19 (poor viability) — building on authority instead of evidence.

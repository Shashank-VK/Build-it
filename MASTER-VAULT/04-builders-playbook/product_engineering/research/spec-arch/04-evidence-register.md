## D. Contradictions & open questions (honest gaps)

- **D1.** Microservices success/failure statistics (73% fail; 42% consolidating) are marketing-adjacent and **Unverified**; first-hand accounts agree on *direction* but not magnitude. No controlled study found in this pass.
- **D2.** Team-size thresholds for microservices are **Unknown** — accounts range from 11 (regretted) to 18 (succeeded with 2 services). Real variables are deploy contention, scaling-profile divergence, ops maturity.
- **D3.** Database QPS crossover heuristics (<100K writes → SQL) are interview-guide rules of thumb, **Unverified** as measured.
- **D4.** SDD marketing figures ("81% code quality improvement") are **Unverified**; the underlying practices (numbered FRs, traceability, gates) are **Corroborated**.
- **D5.** AGENTS.md adoption counts (20K/60K) are industry-guide claims, **Unverified** precise; the cross-tool convention status is **Corroborated**.
- **D6.** Most failure accounts are pseudonymous Medium posts (single-source, survivorship-biased toward dramatic reversions). Patterns recurring across 5+ independent accounts are treated as **Corroborated**; individual metrics as illustrative only.
- **D7.** Caching/observability *cost* specifics and queue *throughput* numbers vary by vendor and date — treated as **Unknown**/changing; principles recorded instead.
- **D8.** This pass did not deeply cover: data-modeling specifics (event sourcing vs CRUD), API versioning strategy, or mobile/offline architectures — flagged as follow-up scope if needed.
